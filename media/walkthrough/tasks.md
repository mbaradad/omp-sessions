# Queue tasks

Add tasks (with subtasks: `Title :: step one; step two`) in the **Tasks** view, with `/queue …`, or **Alt+Enter** in the composer. Drag to reorder — even across sessions — and tick to complete. **Start Queue** and omp works through them one by one in the background; a finished or failed task, or a question, marks the session **● new** until you look.

**Switchyard: Open Overview** (the pulse button in the Sessions view title) shows all of it on one page: what needs you, what runs, what starts next and when, what waits for another task, what is held by a provider limit, a usage window or the budget (and why), and the scheduler's limit, pause switch and spend.
